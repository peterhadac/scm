---
title: Aeropress
description: Coffee-to-water ratios for brewing with an Aeropress.
---

A 1:15 coffee-to-water ratio (15g coffee per 225ml water) for a standard single serving. Use a medium-fine grind and steep for about 1–2 minutes before pressing.

| Servings / Cups | Coffee (g) | Water (ml) |
| ---------------: | ---------: | ---------: |
| 1  | 15  | 225  |
| 2  | 30  | 450  |
| 3  | 45  | 675  |
| 4  | 60  | 900  |
