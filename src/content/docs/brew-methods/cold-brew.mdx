---
title: Cold Brew
description: Coffee-to-water ratios for brewing cold brew.
---

A roughly 1:14 coffee-to-water ratio, scaled by batch size. Use a coarse grind and steep in the refrigerator for 12–24 hours, then strain.

| Batch Size | Coffee (g) | Water (ml) |
| ---------: | ---------: | ---------: |
| 700ml | 50 | 700  |
| 1L    | 70 | 1000 |
