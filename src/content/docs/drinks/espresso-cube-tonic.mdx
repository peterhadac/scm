---
title: Espresso Cube Tonic
description: Espresso ice cubes topped with tonic water.
---

## Ingredients

- 3 espresso ice cubes (equal to one double shot)
- 1.5 dcl (150 ml) tonic water

## Method

1. Freeze brewed espresso into cubes ahead of time — 3 cubes per drink, equivalent to a double shot.
2. Fill a glass with the espresso cubes.
3. Top with 1.5 dcl tonic water.
